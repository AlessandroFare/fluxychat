## Intent

Product showcase launch video for FluxyChat open beta. Dynamic, Apple Keynote energy —
confident, fast cuts, cinematic motion on real product visuals. Primary destination:
LinkedIn and X (16:9 master; cut 9:16 and 1:1 later). Sell the differentiator:
agents live in the same chat timeline with visible tool calls, not bolted-on DMs.

Tone: technical but accessible. Not corporate-slideshow — kinetic type, real UI,
terminal authenticity.
