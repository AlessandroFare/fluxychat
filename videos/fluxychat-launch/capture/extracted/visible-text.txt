[h2] FAQs
[p] Questions we hear when teams compare hosted chat APIs and edge deployments.
[span] What is the chat API vs the SDK?
[p] Your backend talks to the Worker over HTTP. The browser uses @fluxy-chat/sdk to join a room, send, and render. Same product, two surfaces.
[span] Can I keep the frontend on Vercel?
[p] Yes. Lots of teams leave Next on Vercel or Netlify and run chat on Workers + Durable Objects. You skip the platform WebSocket limits and a second Pusher-style bill.
