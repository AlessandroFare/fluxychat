# EU consent & DPA

Rooms with EU members or tenant policy requiring GDPR flows show a **consent banner** on first join. Consent events append to an audit log for DPA evidence.

## Console

**Settings → Consent** — tenant defaults, DPA text references, export consent log.

## In chat

`EuConsentBanner` appears until the user acknowledges. No tracking beyond consent timestamp + version.

## Pairs with

- [Data residency](/docs/guides/enterprise/dlp-audit-export) (region pinning)
- [Ephemeral messages](/docs/guides/ephemeral-messages) (retention minimization)

Not legal advice — configure text with your counsel.
