# 10008 — ws_policy

A 1008 policy close whose reason did not match a named refusal identifier. Treat it as terminal until the reason is known.

**At a glance** — close 1008 (unmapped reason) · terminal · SDK `FluxyAuthError` without a `refusalCode`.

## What to do

- Log the raw close reason text — that is the whole point of this catch-all identifier.
- Most 1008 closes are actually one of the named refusals; check [auth_refused](/docs/errors/auth_refused), [not_member](/docs/errors/not_member), and [token_expired](/docs/errors/token_expired) first.
- The Worker also uses 1008 for `protocol_unsupported` and bare `Unauthorized` / `Forbidden` reasons — those arrive here too.
