# Enterprise sales script

Structure: pain → impact → proof → fit → next step.  
Do not open with a feature list.

---

## Opening

"Thanks for your time. Before I show the product, I'd like to understand how you handle [support / chat / compliance / AI] today and where it costs you the most in time, risk, or tool sprawl."

---

## Discovery questions

1. What triggered you to evaluate a change now?
2. Who will be involved in the decision?
3. Which security/compliance requirements are non-negotiable?
4. What does your approval process look like?
5. What metrics would tell you the project succeeded?

---

## Positioning (after you understand context)

"FluxyChat isn't just chat. It's a self-hosted realtime layer with AI, governance, and omnichannel. It unifies communication, automation, and compliance without vendor lock-in."

---

## Proof (only what matters to them)

Examples to mention when relevant:

- SSO / SCIM and audit trail
- Retention and GDPR export
- MCP and in-room AI actions
- Omnichannel (SMS / WA) in inbox
- Custom domain and embed widget
- Voice + transcription
- Incident response and on-call (pilot / roadmap)

"I can show how this maps to your case in about 10 minutes."

---

## Close

"If there's a fit, the next step is a narrow pilot on one use case. We measure real value and security requirements before expanding."

---

## What to avoid

- Opening with a feature dump
- Talking about Cloudflare architecture before the problem
- Asking "want a demo?" without context
- Treating enterprise like pure self-serve PLG

---

## Dual motion (PLG + sales-led)

**PLG:** quickstart, SDK, public demo, GitHub, guides, Product Hunt / HN.

**Sales-led:** targeted outreach, vertical landing pages, pilot, security pack, case studies, integrator referrals.
